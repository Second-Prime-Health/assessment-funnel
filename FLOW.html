Assessment funnel flow

Positioned as step 1 of the program: the assessment arms the call, the call delivers the answers. Every step in order, top to bottom. White cards are questions. Dark cards are the trust interstitials, and each one is dynamic: it reads the answers just given and shows the matching proof. Gold-edged cards only appear on certain paths.

Contact and routing
Contact

Last step. Who is this assessment for?

Name, email, phone. Submit fires the GHL webhook for everyone (statuses, all answers, internal score for triage) and the pixel event. GHL sends the assessment summary email from this data.

Routing after “logging your assessment…”

Qualified

→ Booking page, immediately (step 2 of 2)

Headline: “Your assessment is in. Pick your time.” Above the calendar: up to 3 plain-language flags from their answers (“the afternoon energy crash,” “family health history”), no score. The pitch: we review your assessment before the call, so it’s about you from minute one. Written summary by email.

Disqualified (below threshold + can’t invest)

→ Assessment summary page, soft path

Full Risk and Performance summary as the free value: their answers mirrored back with what each usually means. Honest “wrong fit” message, no booking CTAs, door stays open.

After booking

Thank-you page

Show-rate homework (accept invite, labs handy, know your one thing) + “see your assessment summary” button. Curiosity gets satisfied after the booking, never instead of it.
